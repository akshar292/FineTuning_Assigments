✅ Task 1 — Features Improved by Fine-Tuning
Instagram ya Zomato jaise apps mein fine-tuning se AI ko specific task ke liye better train kiya ja sakta hai.
1. Zomato Food Recommendation
Fine-tuning se AI ko food preferences aur restaurant-related examples par train kiya ja sakta hai. Isse AI user ki पसंद ke according Gujarati, Punjabi, South Indian, Jain, spicy, etc. food recommendations ko better understand kar sakta hai.
2. Instagram Content Recommendation
AI ko user engagement aur content-related data ke examples par fine-tune karke relevant posts, reels aur content categories ko better identify karne mein help mil sakti hai.
3. Customer Support Chatbot
Zomato customer-support chatbot ko restaurant, delivery, refund aur order-related conversations par fine-tune kiya ja sakta hai. Isse chatbot app ke specific terminology aur common customer questions ko better handle kar sakta hai.
