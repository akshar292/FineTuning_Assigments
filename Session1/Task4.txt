Scenario
Ek music app Gujarat ke users ko regional songs recommend karna chahta hai.
Options:
- Prompting
- RAG
- Fine-Tuning
Choice: RAG
Is scenario mein RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) useful approach ho sakta hai, especially agar regional song information frequently update hoti rahe.
Reason 1 — Domain-specific information
RAG music catalog se current information retrieve kar sakta hai, jaise:
Gujarati songs
Gujarati artists
Garba songs
Dayro
Regional albums

Reason 2 — Updated information
Music catalog frequently change ho sakta hai. RAG external/current data retrieve kar sakta hai without repeatedly fine-tuning the model.
Important Difference
Prompting → Instructions
RAG       → External/current information
Fine-Tuning → Model behavior/style/task adaptation

Note: Agar primary goal highly personalized recommendation ranking hai, to recommendation system/user-history models bhi typically important honge; RAG alone complete recommendation system nahi hai.
