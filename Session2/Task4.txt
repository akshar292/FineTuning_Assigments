| Criteria | Prompt Engineering | RAG | Fine-Tuning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Setup Cost** | Low | Medium | Higher |
| **Time to Deploy** | Very fast | Moderate | Longer |
| **Maintenance** | Low to moderate | Moderate to high because data needs updating | Higher because model may need retraining/re-evaluation |
| **Ideal Use Cases** | Instructions, tone, formatting, simple behavior changes | Current/domain-specific information | Consistent task behavior, style, or domain adaptation |
| **Need Training Data?** | No | Requires knowledge source/data | Requires suitable training examples |
| **Knowledge Updates** | Not directly | Easy by updating the knowledge source | Usually requires additional training |
| **Example** | Instagram bio generator | Product-support chatbot | Specialized support-response behavior |
