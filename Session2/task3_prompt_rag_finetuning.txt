1. Prompt Engineering — Zomato Chatbot
Scenario: User asks:
"Suggest me a vegetarian dinner."

Prompt engineering useful hai because we can give the chatbot instructions about response format, tone, dietary preferences, etc.
User Question
      ↓
Detailed Prompt
      ↓
AI Response

Why? Simple behavior customization ke liye model ko retrain karne ki zarurat nahi hoti.
2. RAG — Flipkart Product Support
Scenario: User asks:
"What is the return policy for this product?"

RAG useful hai because product information and return policies can change. The chatbot can retrieve the latest information from the relevant database/document and use it to answer.
User Question
      ↓
Search Product Information
      ↓
Retrieve Relevant Data
      ↓
AI Answer

3. Fine-Tuning — Paytm Customer Support
Scenario: Paytm wants a chatbot that consistently follows a particular customer-support response style and handles many examples of support conversations.
Fine-tuning can be considered when many high-quality examples are available and the desired behavior needs to be learned consistently rather than repeatedly specified in prompts.
Support Conversations
        ↓
Fine-Tuning
        ↓
Customized Model
        ↓
Customer Support
